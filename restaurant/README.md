# Momo's Little Restaurant

An English speaking adventure for 6-year-olds, made for **Ready Steady 2A – Unit 4: The Restaurant**.
Chef Momo (a little panda) opens his restaurant; the children help him in 12 missions (one golden plate each on the map).

| # | Mission | Language |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the Menu | pasta, rice, fish, chicken, salad, apple pie, a slice of cake, water, orange juice |
| 2 | Hungry Customers | What do you want? – I want fish and salad, please. |
| 3 | Here You Are! | Here you are. – Thank you! |
| 4 | Eat or Drink? | What do you want to eat / to drink? |
| 5 | Yummy! | It's sweet! It's delicious! It's tasty! |
| 6 | Count with Momo | ten, eleven, twelve |
| 7 | What's Missing? | food words |
| 8 | Memory Game | food words |
| 9 | Table Manners | Water, please! Thank you! We use table manners! |
| 10 | The Little Red Hen | Who will help me? – Not I! / I will! |
| 11 | My Favourite Food | What's your favourite food / drink? – My favourite food is chicken. |
| 12 | Restaurant Party | the whole dialogue · Little Chef certificate |

The customers are the family characters of Unit 2; the Little Red Hen story uses the farm animals of Unit 3.

* **Classroom mode** (`?mode=class`, set by the units menu): all missions open, the class answers together first, then Momo calls one child by name from the class list chosen on the main site. No microphone (smart boards have none).
* **Home mode**: missions open one after another; optional microphone button (the recording stays in the browser tab).
* Voices: recorded with the open Kokoro TTS model – Momo `af_bella`, women `af_kore`, men `am_michael`; every clip was checked with two speech recognisers (Parakeet and Whisper). Children's names use the device voice.
* Pictures: generated in Canva for this game.
* No build step: open `index.html` (works from a web server or straight from disk). Progress is saved only in this browser (`momo-restaurant:v1`).
